This is QuickNotes: the fastest way to capture what you need to remember from a meeting.
Say you're on a call, and someone asks you to follow up on something. Don't switch apps. Just press Command, Shift, N.
A note box pops up right where you are. Type the action item, press Enter, and it's saved to today. Your meeting never leaves the screen.
Later, open QuickNotes on the web, your phone, or your Mac. Everything you captured is waiting in today's list.
Tick things off as you finish them.
Drag tasks up or down to set your priorities.
Didn't get to something? Move it to tomorrow in one click. Overdue days are flagged, so nothing slips.
Need a teammate on it? Share the note by email.
It works on your phone too, with dark mode, and everything syncs live.
In Chrome, press Option, Shift, N, or right-click any text to save it.
